ANGI on the Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W

1. Flash the image to a 16 GB or larger microSD card.
2. Open the card's FAT boot partition on your computer. Copy an OpenSSH
   Ed25519 public key there and name the copy angi-ssh.pub. Generate a key
   with ssh-keygen -t ed25519 if needed. Keep the private key on your computer.
3. Eject the card, insert it into the Pi, and connect the Pi's micro-USB
   data port to your computer with a data cable. Power the Pi.
4. Set your computer's USB Ethernet adapter to 10.79.0.1/24 with no gateway.
   Connect to root@10.79.0.2 using the private key and run agx tui. Trust the
   board's new SSH host key using the direct connection.
5. In Profile, create your ANGI account and set its password. In Network,
   configure your country and connect to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network.
6. Open an SSH tunnel to the desktop from your computer:

   ssh -NT -L 127.0.0.1:5943:127.0.0.1:5900 root@10.79.0.2

   Point your VNC viewer at 127.0.0.1:5943 and sign in with the ANGI account
   you created. You can also tunnel to the Pi's Wi-Fi address once connected.
